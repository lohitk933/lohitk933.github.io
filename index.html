Basket Assist Prototype

Basket Assist, concept prototype

Built by Lohit Kumar for a PM capstone. A concept, not the real Zepto app. Products, prices and history are mock data. Pick a situation, then use the phone.

The rules this prototype follows

  1. Suggestions sit under the cart, never on top of it, and never stop checkout.
  2. Three tiles at most. No timers, no stock scarcity, no pre-ticked items.
  3. One item in the cart? The strip collapses to a quiet link. You are not penalised.
  4. "I'm in a hurry" turns suggestions off for next time. One tap.
  5. Removing is as easy as adding: the same stepper, plus Undo.
  6. Sponsored items live in their own labelled box and never appear as a suggestion.
  7. No progress bar to a free-delivery threshold. The delivery fee does not change when you add things.
